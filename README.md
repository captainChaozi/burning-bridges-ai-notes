# Burning Bridges AI notes

Practical notes for [Burning Bridges AI](https://burningbridges.dev/), a one-photo AI video generator that places you as the lead in an original chandelier-party performance.

## What it makes

- 10-second, 720p MP4, 16:9 landscape or 9:16 portrait, silent.
- Three prepared gestures: wave, cup toast, hand-over-eyes search.
- No filming, editing or prompt writing.

## Photo requirements

- One consenting adult, clear unobstructed face; waist-up helps.
- JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB, at least 128 × 128 px.
- No children, unauthorised public figures or misleading impersonation.
